## Campaign outline
This is a rough outline just hitting the main story beats, and explaining perhaps why they happen.

- Avitus has acquired permission from amongs fellow Armada leadership to make a machine mind with his desired parameters, with the caveat that it must be prepared and taught to participate in the war (they have lost multiple commanders lately and have need)
  - this need is less real and mostly percieved, as Armada probanly holds at least 2/3rds of the galaxy at this time. Cortex, being a splinter faction that has frequent internal conflict, simply hasn't been able to dominate much more than their initial surprise conquest.
- Avitus, however, decides to use the most minimal memory imprint possible, only providing the information required to be able to counicate. This had fallen out of practice as new and diverse perspectives had created so much conflict that Armada more often acted out of need, and even then most of history and other knowledge was often imprinted. Avitus decides not to do this, to give as unbiased a beginning he can. This new mind, is you, the player. 
  - this is a really good start, as it means Avitus and other characters would naturally assume a level of ignorance.
- Howevwr, as was the agreement he made, Avitus must trainnyou for war and conflict. so he does. (this gives a few tutorial style missions.)

This vindicates Catreus' ideas, and this pleases him so much that he offers to preserve your name as a great warrior, and asks for your name. you, the character, reply that you have not chosen one.
