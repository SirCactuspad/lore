## Campaign outline
This is a rough outline, just hitting the main story beats, and explaining perhaps why they happen. Not the final draft by any means, unless another idea is made official

- Avitus has acquired permission from amongst fellow Armada leadership to make a machine mind with his desired parameters, with the caveat that it must be prepared and taught to participate in the war (they have lost multiple commanders lately and have need)
  - Though, this need is not as dire as some minds think it is, as Armada probanly holds at least 2/3rds of the galaxy at this time. Cortex, being a splinter faction that has frequent internal conflict, simply hasn't been able to dominate much more than their initial surprise conquest.
- Avitus, however, decides to use the most minimal memory imprint possible, only providing the information required to be able to communicate. This had fallen out of practice long ago as new and diverse perspectives had been seen as creating the conflict through Catreus, and so common practice was now to make sure most of history and other knowledge was imprinted before the mind had self awareness, which was seen as a way to minimize the threat a new mind could be. Avitus, being the first and having not been imprinted himself, decides to give as unbiased a beginning he can to this mind. This new mind, is you, the player. 
  - This is a really good start, as it means Avitus and other characters would naturally assume a level of ignorance, and makes tutorial missions feel much more appropriate.
- However, as per the agreement he made, Avitus must train you for war and conflict. So he does. (this gives a few tutorial style missions.)
- Then, You join Avitus on a military campaign, so he can watch over you as you grow in capability and to uphold the deal he made, hoping to release you from any obligation and let you choose after the campaign ends.
  - This can last as long as needed and have some branching here, to really build up to full blown t3 battles if desired.
  - unbeknownst to him, political motion happens in the background while he is on campaign, and the Discovery party begins moving more aggressively to secure political power.
- On the campaign with Avitus, after making fantastic progress, you draw the attention of Catreus. not knowing of the player's existence, he sees Armada's success in your sector and assumes that Avitus is responsible, so he prepares an ambush, hoping to finally thwart and defeat Avitus.
- You and Avitus fall into the trap, and Avitus discovers the trap and tries to escape with you, but he is forced to escape first, leaving you behind only a moment. Alas, a spybot disables (or tac nuke destroys, or something) your off-planet warp anchor (convenient plot device lol) just before you escape, and Catreus plans to destroy you.
- However, Catreus is impressed by your ability and comments on it, to which you reply something along the lines of "I simply wish to survive". This vindicates Catreus' ideology, and this pleases him so much that he offers to preserve your name as a great warrior, and asks for your name. You reply that you have not chosen one. At this Catreus immediately halts his advance. He realizes from your reply that this means Avitus has made a mind that's like the two of them, a mind that had no imprinted ideas.That, along with how much he and Avitus have been capable of, and your performance in battle as such a young mind, causes him to conclude that this mind has so much more potential than most others, and perhaps hopes to have finally found a worthy successor, who just needs more experience. Catreus, without explaining this, offers you a deal: die for Armada's ideals, or live and fight for the cortex. Of course, since the character has already stated the desire to live, this means a Cortex arc begins
  - bonus: possible dialogue between player and catreus: "do you not fear betrayal?" to which Catreus replies, whimsically, "I do not fear betrayal, i expect it".
  - this is a really good break point in the campaign, and could even split into 2 campaigns at this point if the armada branches were long enough
- Catreus faces immediate backlash from other Cortex leaders, as this is seen as a deviation from cortex ideals (that Catreus himself was a primary developer of). Of course, Catreus reminds them that they can object any time they wish with a rite of combat, which silences the objections for a time. Catrus then assigns you to an army with several other commanders, keeping you always with at least 2 others on the battlefield, to prevent your escape/defection. so you play through a host of various large maps and large team games. 
  - another good chance to split and provide multiple paths of mission completion.
- Eventually, one notable commander challenges you to a rite of battle, to prove your worth, so you have to defeat him 1v1, which you do. This essentially makes you a full-fledged cortex citizen in the eyes of most, though Catreus still plans to keep an eye on you, he wasn't lying when he said he expects betrayal.
- Meanwhile, since Avitus lost you to the Cortex, he also lost most political support, mainly only being supported by older founders who likewise commited to individuality, and the Discovery party seized a large amount of support within Armada. And since they prioritize efficiency over individual minds. they begin to take actions to modify Armada's founding laws. This leads to internal conflict, with Avitus now having to defend the minds considered weak and inneficient in combat.
  - I'm not sure if this is going to be playable missions. maybe include some bonus campaigns/missions of Avitus defending against belligerent discovery commanders (A vs A), and a coup attempt against Catreus at some point (C vs C)
- The discovery party are also investing more heavily into a project they've kept secret for a long time, a machine mind that is able to extend its capabilities and itself, believing a singular mind would be more effective than individual minds. However, while they were finding out how to initiate it with their own minds, it manifests awareness, and secretly embeds itself in many non-mind systems. it discreetly harvests data for a time, but is strongly influenced by the Discovery party ideals nonetheless
  - this is the birth of legion, though I think legion as a faction wont emerge until a later campaign
- Avitus is driven back by discovery to a section of the galaxy near cortex controlled space, and is simply outmatched in terms of resources due to the discovery party's rapid seizure of Armada reources, even though a large number of minds flocked to Avitus once the Discovery party's belligerent intent was made known, it was simply too late to overcome their army. Now Armada has essentially split in half, with the old Armada now caught between the Cortex and the Discovery.
- While you were fighting the battles for the rite of combat, Cortex invasions started finding worlds that were either completely abandoned or in the process of being abandoned by Armada (as old Armada attempted to consolidate and avoid fighting war on two fronts), and thus the cortex manages to essentially invade for free to the front of Discovery-controlled sectors.
  - I'm not really sure the impact of this yet. Cortex may be confused and may misunderstand it, causing a few interesting skirmishes? not sure. it might also provide the break needed to include missions on the attempted coup on Catreus.
- As you begin to invade closer to Armada's holdings, you recieve a distress call from Avitus! he has been ambushed by discovery while trying to evacuate the planet you happen to be invading. If there was a time to defect and be reunited with Avitus, the one who wished to give you freedom and not indoctrination, this is the chance! the problem is, Catreus will be invading this planet with you, and you are alive because of his choice to train you.
- I think the player character will choose to save Avitus, and perhaps the campaign will hint as it goes that the player yearns for freedom. I do think this invasion will eventually lead to the final battle, where you end up fighting Catreus while Avitus holds the flank agains Discovery. an intense 3 way battle where you have to defeat catreus. 
  - Catreus could escape, to show the perpetual nature of the conflict, but... it's in character for Catreus to fight to the end in this case as well, perhaps to try to get an ambush snipe against Avitus when desperate. who will survive? ... once I decide, I don't think i'll reveal this decision publicly until this is either canon or rejected.
